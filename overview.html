Interpretation guide

What these counts mean

Agent-dayOne agent’s observed activity on one calendar day. This is the unit classified in Stage 1.
Goal periodAll analyzed days for one agent under the same recorded goal. It is the denominator used for the 14.6% confirmed-drift share.
Confirmed episodeA bounded or still-ongoing interval accepted by Stage 2 as goal drift, with a recorded onset, activity narrative, and supporting evidence.
