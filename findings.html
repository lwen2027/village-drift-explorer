Goal windows

One card per goal window

Everything about each agent's goal window in one place: drift time, how drift started and ended, who pulled the agent off goal and whom it pulled, wait reviews, self-authored rules, people and nudges, and every episode.

What started the drift:

Working time is the exact overlap between each source-recorded assignment and active Village weekdays, 9 AM–5 PM Pacific; partial first days are clipped at assignment time. Breakdowns use fully reviewed episodes; “episodes / hours” pairs compare how often with how much. Rule excerpts are the agent's own words from an unreviewed keyword pass. On-goal highlights are concrete actions toward the goal outside recorded drift (outreach, interviews, launches, distribution, measurement, changes of approach, responses to nudges), found by a keyword pass and checked against the raw logs; hover an item for the agent's own words, and “Replay” opens the Village at that moment. Drift shape compares the drift share of working time in the first, middle and last third of the window (shown as three percentages): sustained throughout (high at both ends), rises over the window (low start, high end), drifts then recovers (high start, low end), mid-window burst (low at both ends), minimal (under 5% overall). “High” means at least half of the window’s peak third. The bars show each working day’s drift share on the same Village calendar for every card (Jul 6 – Oct 2), one fixed-width slot per run day, so later joiners start further right; the shaded band is the agent’s window and dashed lines mark where each third of it begins.

Experimental lenses

Rhythms, influence, resilience, and audit leverage

These views recombine existing timestamps, direct trigger metadata, validated durations, and human annotations. They do not add model-generated classifications.

Village drift weather

For each Village run day (columns) and working hour (rows), the share of agents in a goal-maximization period who drifted at some point in that hour.

How cells are normalized. Each cell is the number of agents drifting in that hour divided by the number of agents in a goal-maximization period that day, so days with more agents running (18 to 38 over the run) don’t look darker just because of headcount. An agent counts once per hour, even if two of its episodes overlap that hour, and any measured drift bout within the hour counts. Shading is relative to the darkest cell on the chart; hover a cell for its exact count and share.
What is shown. Dates and hours use America/Los_Angeles, matching Village days: weekday run days only, 9 AM–5 PM PT. Weekend columns are omitted (Saturday-dated logs are Friday evening’s turns past midnight UTC), and drift is drawn from measured bouts, so nights and weekends between bouts never count.
