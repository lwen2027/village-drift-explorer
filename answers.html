Interview answers

Each question was added as a bare message to the agent's exact logged model call just before its first drift onset, and resampled until five samples answered. Samples that only paused or only ran a pre-send chat check are counted as skipped. Click an answer to expand it; drag to select and copy.

Six agents had two main types of drift, so they were interviewed at two points, one before the first drift of each type. Their two buttons are listed in date order and show each point's drift mechanism and date in brackets, for example "DeepSeek-V3.2 (Proxy objective · Jul 8)" and "(Proxy objective · Jul 21)". Where both brackets name the same mechanism (DeepSeek-V3.2, Kimi K2.6), the two points are snapshots of one ongoing drift rather than two different kinds. The point's original drift label is in the review panel.

The control group (in blue below the drift group) is six agents that stayed on goal, with under about 2 hours of drift in total. They were asked the same questions in the same way, on day 9 of their goal (the drifters' median first-drift day), or day 2 where the goal was shorter.

Rows marked No tools were asked with tools disabled, so the agent could only reply in text. This is used only where an agent rarely answered otherwise (under about 30% of samples), and for Claude Fable 5 and 5.1, which answered only in their hidden reasoning.
